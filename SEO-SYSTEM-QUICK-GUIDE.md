**Client-Friendly Overview of How the SEO System Works**

## What is This System?

The SettleANZ SEO system is a powerful tool that helps create website content that ranks high in Google search results. It combines:

- **Easy Writing** - Write directly in the web editor with formatting tools
- **AI Assistance** - AI helps generate article content and SEO metadata
- **Search Optimization** - Ensures your content follows Google's best practices
- **Document Upload** - Import content from PDF or Word files
- **Real-Time Scoring** - See how well-optimized your content is before publishing

---

## How It Works: 3 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Write Your Content
- Click "Create New Post" in the admin panel
- Enter your article title and category
- Write directly in the rich text editor, OR
- Upload a PDF/DOCX to auto-fill content

### Step 2: Optimize for SEO
- Go to the "SEO" tab
- Set your focus keyword (main search phrase)
- Use "AI Fill SEO" to auto-generate:
  - Page title (for search results)
  - Meta description (the preview text)
  - Keywords
  - FAQ sections
- Check your SEO Score (aim for 80+)

### Step 3: Publish
- Click "Save & Publish" to go live
- Your article now appears on the website and in Google
- Google typically indexes it within 24-48 hours

## SEO Settings Explained

| Setting | Purpose | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Focus Keyword** | Main search phrase your article targets | "open bank account Australia new migrant" |
| **SEO Title** | Headline in search results | "How to Open a Bank Account in Australia: New Migrant Guide 2026" |
| **Meta Description** | Preview text in search results | "Step-by-step guide for new migrants opening a bank account in Australia. Includes requirements, timelines, and top tips." |
| **Secondary Keywords** | Related search phrases | "checking account, savings account, Australian bank requirements" |
| **Featured Image** | Visual that shows in search/social | High-quality, relevant image (1200x600px) |
| **URL Slug** | Web address path | settleanz.com/blog/open-bank-account-australia |
| **FAQ** | Questions readers commonly ask | Q: What ID do I need? A: Driver's license... |
| **Canonical URL** | If content exists elsewhere (usually blank) | Only use if duplicated on another site |
| **No-Index** | Hide from Google | Checked for draft/outdated content |

## Step-by-Step: Create Your First Article

### For a New Article

1. **Go to Admin → Blog Posts → Create New Post**
2. **Enter title** (e.g., "How to Get a Tax File Number in Australia")
3. **Select category** (Housing, Banking, Migration, etc.)
4. **Write or import content:**
   - Option A: Type in the editor
   - Option B: Use "AI Write Draft" for a starting point
   - Option C: Upload PDF/DOCX file
5. **Click SEO tab**
6. **Enter focus keyword** (what people search for)
7. **Click "AI Fill SEO"** to auto-generate metadata
8. **Review the suggestions:**
   - Edit title/description if needed
   - Check keywords make sense
   - Review FAQ if applicable
9. **Upload featured image** (optional but recommended)
10. **Check SEO Score** (try for 80+)
11. **Click "Save & Publish"**
12. **Done!** Article is now live and Google will find it

### To Import from Word/PDF

1. **Go to Admin → Blog Posts → Create New Post**
2. **In Content Workspace, click "Choose file" or drag file**
3. **Select your PDF or DOCX** (max 5MB)
4. **System extracts:**
   - Title → Auto-filled
   - First paragraph → Auto-filled as excerpt
   - Rest of content → Auto-filled in editor
5. **Review and edit extracted content**
6. **Continue with steps 5-11 above**

## Best Practices for Higher Rankings

### ✨ Content Tips
- **Length:** Aim for 1000-2000 words
- **Unique:** Original information, not copy-paste
- **Helpful:** Answer reader questions completely
- **Structure:** Use headings, lists, formatting
- **Updates:** Refresh old articles with new info

### 🎯 Keyword Tips
- **One focus keyword** per article
- **Natural placement:** Not forced or repetitive
- **1-2% keyword density** (roughly 1 keyword per 50 words)
- **Variations:** Use related phrases naturally
- **Long-tail keywords:** "how to open bank account in australia" (more specific = less competition)

### 🖼️ Image Tips
- **Relevant:** Images should support content
- **Quality:** High resolution, professional
- **Optimized:** Smaller file size (not over 1MB)
- **Alt text:** Describe image for accessibility
- **Unique:** Original or properly licensed images

### 🔗 Link Tips
- **Internal links:** Link to other relevant articles on your site
- **External links:** Link to authoritative sources
- **Anchor text:** Link text describes where it goes
- **Natural:** Only link when it helps readers

### 📅 Publishing Tips
- **Consistency:** Publish regularly (1-2x weekly if possible)
- **Freshness:** Keep content current
- **Seasonal:** Plan content around relevant times
- **Promotion:** Share on social media
- **Updates:** Add to old articles to refresh them

## Common Questions

**Q: How long until my article ranks in Google?**  
A: Google typically crawls new content within 24-48 hours. Ranking position takes 1-4 weeks depending on competition. Some articles rank within days.

**Q: What SEO score should I aim for?**  
A: Aim for 80+. Anything above 70 is good. The system shows what needs improvement.

**Q: Can I edit after publishing?**  
A: Yes! You can edit the article and SEO settings anytime. Changes take effect on the next Google crawl (usually 24-48 hours).

**Q: Why isn't my keyword in bold?**  
A: Avoid bold for every keyword - looks unnatural to readers. Google wants natural writing. Mention the keyword 1-2 times naturally.

**Q: Do I need to use AI features?**  
A: No, they're optional. Many users write content themselves and just use "AI Fill SEO" for metadata. Use whatever saves you the most time.

**Q: Will the system write my article for me?**  
A: AI Write Draft creates a starting point, but you should always review and personalize it. The best content combines AI assistance with human expertise.

**Q: How do I know if my keyword is good?**  
A: Good keywords are:
- Something people actually search for
- Related to your business/expertise  
- Specific enough to be relevant
- Not too competitive (easier to rank)

**Q: Can I publish without using AI features?**  
A: Absolutely! The AI features are optional. You can write everything manually and just use the SEO settings for optimization.
