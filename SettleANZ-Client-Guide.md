## Welcome

SettleANZ is a modern website built to welcome people who are **new to Australia**. It combines helpful guides, expert articles, trusted partner listings, and easy ways for visitors to get in touch — all managed from a simple **Admin Dashboard** that your team can use without technical skills.

This guide explains **what your website does for visitors** (the public site) and **how your team manages it** (the admin area). It is written in plain language so clients, marketers, and content editors can understand every major feature.

## 1. What SettleANZ Does

SettleANZ helps migrants and newcomers:

- **Plan their arrival** with checklists and practical guides  
- **Compare services** such as banking, housing, and migration support  
- **Find trusted partners** in a searchable business directory  
- **Read expert articles** on the blog  
- **Reach your team** through forms, chat, and WhatsApp  

Your business benefits from:

- **More qualified leads** captured in one place  
- **Full control over content** without calling a developer for every update  
- **Strong search visibility** with built-in SEO tools  
- **A professional, mobile-friendly** brand experience

## 2. Public Website — Pages & Features

These are the pages your visitors see. All are mobile-friendly and use your brand colours (teal, sand, and accent orange).

### Home Page (`/`)

The main landing page. It typically includes:

- **Hero section** — Welcome message and call to action  
- **Why SettleANZ** — Key benefits for newcomers  
- **Founder / owner story** — Builds trust  
- **Testimonials** — Social proof from happy clients  
- **Featured guides** — Links to your most important help content  
- **Partner logos** — Trusted brands you work with  
- **Email capture strip** — Visitors can leave their details  
- **Latest blog posts** — Keeps the site fresh and improves SEO  
- **Country acknowledgement** — Respectful cultural note  

### New to Australia (`/new-to-australia`)

A long, practical **arrival guide** with:

- Table of contents for easy navigation  
- Checklists before you fly  
- First-week priorities  
- FAQs  

Ideal for SEO and for sharing with people planning their move.

### Settlement Services (`/settlement-services`)

Showcases your **paid packages** (e.g. pre-arrival support, airport meet & greet, 90-day help). Visitors can:

- Compare packages and pricing  
- Read FAQs  
- **Book a package** via a popup form (request goes to Admin → Package Requests)

### Housing Guide (`/housing`)

Advice on renting and choosing suburbs, plus **featured relocation partners**.  
*Note: This page may be linked from guides or search rather than the main menu.*

### Banking Guide (`/banking`)

Helps visitors understand **Australian banking** and money transfer options with comparison-style content.

### Migration Services (`/migration-services`)

Covers visa types and connects visitors with **immigration agents** from your directory. Includes:

- Eligibility / consultation form  
- Agent booking form  

### Blog (`/blog` and `/blog/{article}`)

- **Blog index** — All published articles with category filters and search  
- **Article page** — Full story, related articles, FAQs, and sharing-friendly layout  

### Business Directory (`/directory`)

A searchable list of vetted businesses:

- Filter by **keyword**, **city**, and **category**  
- **Featured listings** appear first  
- Each listing has its own detail page  

### Directory Listing Detail (`/directory/{business}`)

Shows business profile, services, contact links, and **customer reviews** (after you approve them).

### Contact (`/contact`)

Contact details (from your settings), a **business listing enquiry** section, and a **contact form** with subjects such as General, Business Listing, Partnership, and Media.

### About (`/about`)

Your story, values, and mission — builds brand trust.

### Legal Pages

- **Privacy Policy** (`/privacy-policy`)  
- **Terms of Service** (`/terms-of-service`)  

Both support SEO settings from the admin panel.

### Automatic SEO Files

- **Sitemap** (`/sitemap.xml`) — Helps Google find your pages  
- **Robots.txt** (`/robots.txt`) — Tells search engines what to crawl (admin area is hidden)

## 3. How Visitors Contact You

All forms send enquiries to your **Admin → Leads** inbox. You do **not** need separate email tools for basic capture (notifications appear inside the admin panel).

| Where on the site | What the visitor does | Where it appears in Admin |
|-------------------|-------------------------|---------------------------|
| Home page strip | Subscribes / asks for guide | All Leads |
| Site-wide popup | Leaves name & email | All Leads |
| Settlement Services | Books a package | Package Requests |
| Contact page | Sends a message | Contact Submissions |
| Migration page | Requests consultation | Book Consultations |
| AI chat (if enabled) | Shares details in conversation | All Leads |

**WhatsApp** — Click-to-chat links in the footer and contact areas (URLs set in Site Settings).

**What you receive per lead:** Name, email, phone (if provided), message, form type, which page they came from, and timestamp. Your team updates status (New → Reviewing → Contacted → Qualified → Closed) and adds internal notes.

## 4. Blog & Content Marketing

The blog is your **content engine** for SEO and trust.

### What visitors see

- Article cards with image, category, title, excerpt, author, date  
- Category filters and search on the blog index  
- Full article with hero image, body content, and related posts  

### What your team can do (Admin → Blog Posts)

| Task | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Create articles | Title, category, excerpt, full body |
| Upload images | Featured image for cards and article header |
| Import content | Upload PDF or Word document to auto-fill draft |
| AI writing help | Generate draft text and SEO fields (optional) |
| Publish / draft | Control what is live on the site |
| Feature on home | Highlight a post on the homepage |
| SEO per post | Meta title, description, social preview, keywords, FAQ |

**Tip:** Use clear, helpful titles (e.g. “Opening a Bank Account in Australia: Step-by-Step”) and one strong featured image per post.

## 5. Business Directory & Reviews

### Directory listings

Each listing can include:

- Business name, category, city  
- Short and full description  
- Services offered  
- Phone, email, website, WhatsApp, booking link  
- Logo / image  
- **Featured** flag (shows prominently)  
- **Published** on/off  

### Reviews

Visitors can submit a **star rating and comment** on a listing page. Reviews stay **pending** until you **approve** them in Admin → Reviews — so only quality feedback goes live.

## 6. AI Assistant (Optional)

A floating **SettleANZ AI Assistant** can answer visitor questions using your site content.

**Controlled in Admin → API Integration Settings:**

- Turn assistant on or off  
- Custom title, greeting, and personality (system prompt)  
- Connect your OpenAI API key  
- Optional web search for broader answers  

When visitors share contact details in chat, a lead can be created for your team to follow up.

## 8. Managing Leads & Enquiries

### Typical workflow

1. Open **All Leads** (or a filtered view).  
2. Click a lead to read full details.  
3. Update **status** and add **internal notes**.  
4. Contact the person by email or phone.  
5. Mark as **Closed** when done.  

### Lead statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| New | Just arrived — needs first response |
| Reviewing | Someone is looking at it |
| Contacted | You have reached out |
| Qualified | Good prospect |
| Closed | Finished (won or lost) |

## 9. Managing Blog Posts

### Create a new post

1. Go to **Blog Posts** → **Create**.  
2. Enter title, category, excerpt, and body.  
3. **Upload featured image** (appears on blog cards and article page).  
4. Fill SEO fields (or use **Generate SEO** if available).  
5. Choose **Publish** or save as **Draft**.  
6. Optionally tick **Feature on homepage**.  

### Edit or unpublish

- Use **Edit** on any post.  
- Quick actions: Publish, Unpublish, Feature, Remove from featured.  

### Filters

View **All**, **Draft**, **Published**, or **Featured** posts only.

---

## 10. Managing Directory Listings

1. Go to **Directory Listings** → **Create** or **Edit**.  
2. Fill business details and contact links.  
3. Set **Published** to show on the public directory.  
4. Set **Featured** to prioritise in search results and on guide pages.  

Categories include areas such as Immigration, Relocation, Finance, Real Estate, Healthcare, and Schools.

---

## 11. Managing Reviews

1. Go to **Reviews**.  
2. Read pending submissions.  
3. Click **Approve** to publish on the listing page, or **Reject** / **Delete** if inappropriate.  

Only approved reviews are visible to the public.

## 12. SEO Manager

Help Google and social media show your pages correctly — **no coding required**.

For each main page (Home, New to Australia, Settlement Services, Blog, About, Contact, Directory, Privacy, Terms) you can set:

- **Page title** — Appears in browser tab and search results  
- **Meta description** — Short summary under your link on Google  
- **Social preview** — Title, description, and image when shared on Facebook/LinkedIn  
- **Focus keywords** — What you want to rank for  

Blog posts have their own SEO fields on each article.

**Also automatic:** XML sitemap and robots.txt.

For a **deeper SEO manual**, download **SEO System Documentation** from Admin (if enabled on your install).

## 16. Tips for Your Team

1. **Respond to leads within 24 hours** — speed builds trust with people relocating under stress.  
2. **Publish one blog article per month** — steady content helps search rankings.  
3. **Keep directory listings accurate** — update phone numbers and links when partners change.  
4. **Approve only genuine reviews** — quality over quantity.  
5. **Check SEO titles** — keep under ~60 characters; descriptions under ~160 characters.  
6. **Use featured images** on every blog post — articles with images get more clicks.  
7. **Test forms after any site update** — submit a test enquiry and confirm it appears in Admin.
