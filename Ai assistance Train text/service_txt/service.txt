 Arrive  The first stage covers everything before and immediately after you land. Most people underestimate how much preparation matters — the wrong paperwork, the wrong suburb, the wrong sequence of steps in the first week can cost you months. This stage makes sure your arrival is the beginning of something good, not the start of a stressful scramble.

Pre-arrival document review
We review your visa, qualifications, references and flag exactly what's missing before you land.

Personalised 90-day action plan
Your specific steps, in the right order — matched to your visa type, city, family, and profession.
Airport meet & greet
Someone who knows Australia or New Zealand meets you at arrivals. SIM sorted, transport handled, neighbourhood briefing done.

Vetted referrals — culturally matched
GP who speaks your language. Accountant who knows your visa. Migration lawyer we personally trust. Not Google results.

Settle
You have found your feet. The adrenaline of arrival is fading and the real work begins finding a permanent home, choosing schools for your children, understanding how banking and healthcare work, and starting to feel like you belong somewhere. This stage handles the practical foundations that make everything else possible. 
Rental finding  on the ground
We attend open homes, send video walkthroughs, and submit applications on your behalf. No rental history? We know how to handle that.
School placement  culturally matched
Not just school ratings. Which school has your community, has ESL support, and fits your children's specific needs.
Banking and financial setup
TFN or IRD, bank account, superannuation or KiwiSaver — in the right order, with the right providers for your situation.
Community & cultural connections
Introductions to your cultural community, mosque, temple, church, or professional network — wherever you belong.

 Work & Invest
You are settled enough to think beyond survival. Now comes the question every migrant eventually asks: how do I get the most out of this country professionally and financially? This stage helps you navigate the job market, understand your earning potential, and start making smart decisions with what you earn, so your future here is as secure as your present. 

Job pathways and recruitment referrals
Referred to vetted recruitment agencies that know how to place skilled migrants ,  not just any recruiter

Credential recognition support
Guidance on NZQA, skills assessment bodies, and professional registration for regulated occupations.

Tax, super and financial planning
Referral to accountants and financial planners who specialise in migrants,  your tax situation is not the same as a local's
Property and business investment
Introduction to property investment, mortgage brokers, buyers agents, and business setup , when you are ready to go beyond earning to building.

Enjoy:
The hardest part is behind you. You have a home, an income, a routine. The stress of arrival has given way to something quieter and more meaningful — a sense that this place is actually yours. Long-term property and wealth planning

Community and cultural deepening
